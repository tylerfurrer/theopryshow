A night of country, played live by the full band with fiddle and steel.

The Opry Show is a night of classic country music. The Rat River Boys play the songs everybody knows, with fiddle and steel guitar, all live.

The night starts where country radio started, with barn dance fiddle tunes and old gospel, then works its way through the honky tonk years, the Nashville sound, the outlaws, the Texas dance halls and the '90s hits that filled every dance floor in America.

The show is built for theaters, dance halls, fairs and festivals. The Rat River Boys are based in Wisconsin and tour nationwide.
